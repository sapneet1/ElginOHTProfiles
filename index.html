About the Data

The Neighbourhood Profiles dashboard brings together information from multiple data sources to support population health planning.

  • Indicators will retain their original source and reporting period.
  • FSA and CSD geography will be maintained separately.
  • Data will be displayed using its appropriate unit and definition.
  • Missing or unavailable values will be clearly identified.
