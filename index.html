About the Data

The Neighbourhood Profiles dashboard brings together multiple data sources to support population health planning.

  • Indicators retain their original reporting periods and sources.
  • FSA and CSD geographies are maintained separately.
  • Data is displayed using the appropriate unit and definition.
  • Missing or unavailable values are clearly identified.
  • Map boundaries are geographic reference areas and do not change the underlying indicator values.
